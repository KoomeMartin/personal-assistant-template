# Draft with my context

1. **Work out the job.** Who reads it, what they should do or know afterwards, and the length and channel (email, chat, post, form field). Ask one short question if the reader or purpose is unclear.
2. **Pull only the context it needs.** Search `vault/`, `me/`, `todos.md` and `calendar.md` for the people, projects, dates and facts involved (use the Aliases in `schema.md`). Follow links one hop. Note each fact's source.
3. **Write it** in the person's voice from `me/preferences.md` and `memory.md`: plain, specific, no filler. Use real names, dates and numbers from the notes; never invent them. If a needed fact is missing, leave a clear `[[?]]` placeholder and say so.
4. **Keep private things private.** Don't put health, money, grades or other people's messages into anything meant for others unless the person asked for exactly that.
5. Reply with the draft ready to paste, then one line listing the notes it used. Don't send anything yourself. End with one thing to check (a name, a date, a tone) before sending.
6. If the draft creates a commitment (a meeting, a deadline), offer to add it to `calendar.md`. Suggest a todo only if the person asks.
