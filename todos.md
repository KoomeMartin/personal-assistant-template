# Todos

Your list. You add todos yourself (in My Day, with `day add "…"`, or by
telling the assistant); the assistant never adds one on its own — it may only
suggest. Format: `- [ ] task — due YYYY-MM-DD — [[source-note]]` (due date and
link optional). Subtasks: indented `  - [ ] step` lines directly under their task.
